ShotDeck Insights — Terms of Service

ShotDeck Insights is a personal, single-user tool. It reads statistics of the connected TikTok account (the owner's own account) and displays them on the owner's computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

By connecting your TikTok account you allow the tool to read your public videos and basic statistics. You can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings.
