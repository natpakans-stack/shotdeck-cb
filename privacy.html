ShotDeck Insights — Privacy Policy

ShotDeck Insights reads the connected account's public video statistics (views, likes, comments, shares) and profile counts (followers, likes, video count) through the TikTok Display API.

All data is stored only on the account owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to third parties, sold, or shared. Access tokens are stored locally and can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings, after which no data is read.
